# Map 3: the Forest

A clearing under a closed canopy. A foggy ravine round a great tree; the tree is the tower, grown for the purpose.
World coordinates, Godot y up; bearings as map 1 (`pol(bearing, r, z)`). Second pass (Ryan on the first: "a very very good first pass").

1. Lane: grass annulus r 46.7..57.3 at y 23.0, gentle 2-D relief +-0.12 m (zero at both edges), painted as fine noise in three gold-olive greens, a worn brown-green path r 50.5..53.5 with a verge one row either side, lane r 52, route 5 deg to 335 deg; a fallen log with a stick fence across the lane at 350 deg (collider box to y 34.5). Unchanged from pass 1 except the fixtures: the bars at 353 deg and the portal at the finish are now THIS map's own, not Map 1's hell rock (Ryan: "now create bars and portals that match the actual maps"). `forest_bars.glb` is the map's own cell bars and fallen-log fence scaled into a gate -- a log sill, two living end posts, a lintel, 20 BRANCHES socketed into the sill's top quads and the lintel's underside, two lashing rails, leaf sprays -- in rock_bars.glb's exact envelope (10.6 x 8.5 m, 0.79 m thick, origin the base centre) and keeping its guarantee that no gap exceeds 0.38 m (widest 0.338 m, bar-bar at z 4.27; the edge slot 0.094 m). Second pass on the bars (Ryan: "the forest bars should look like natural branches, not just bars made of wood. and i mean that in shape not just through some leaves on it"): a bar is no longer a bowed dowel but a branch -- an irregular taper that sheds most of its girth in its first third, its own bow through the thickness, a wander and one or two kinks across the lane, a swelling at each knot, short cut stubs where side branches were taken off, and on every fourth bar a fork near the top whose second limb welds into the lintel's FRONT face (so a fork costs no extra lintel geometry). The lintel's lane boundaries drift across the gate, so a bar's head does not stand over its foot and the screen is a fan, not a comb. Every number comes off the one seed. `forest_portal.glb` is two trunks braided over the opening into a pointed arch with branches and leaf clumps over it, round portal.glb's exact opening (4.5 x 4.0 x 0.8 m envelope, 2.7 m clear at the ground, springing 2.60, apex 3.15, surface centre 1.50, clear gate 2.60 x 2.80), so scenes/ring/forest_portal.tscn is portal.tscn node for node -- same Glow, same Gate, same finish_gate group -- and the effect surface keeps portal_build's own swirl painter verbatim and the same doubleSided emissive material, painted into the forest atlas's spare `earth` cell. 2806 and 1342 visual tris (colliders 312 and 148); each is one contiguous mesh (components 1, duplicate positions 0), and unlike portal.glb's the disc does not float -- it is fanned off the frame's own y=0 vertex row.
2. Pit: the lip at r 46.7 rounds over 1.0 m, then an earth-and-root bank drops to a dark floor at y -11.05 (r 42), no water. Fog: ForestFog, seven translucent discs y -12.5..-8 (alpha 0.5 at the floor, y -11.05, the old water height, easing to 0.06 three metres over it: the fog starts where the floor was and the bank stands clear down to it; zero inside r 6.8 round the trunk so no disc cuts it, zero 0.6 m into the bank so no edge shows; GL Compatibility has no fog volumes, so the fog is layers). KillBox roof at y 0.0, just over the thicket's tallest thorns, r 62, 30 m deep. The thicket: the floor is a grid of quads (rings r 42, 38 in to 11 by 1.5 at 120 columns, r 7 at 60, a centre cap under the trunk) and ~1140 barbed brambles out of single-quad floor sockets between r 11 and 38, roots 1.0 m apart, zone "cell" so they read as black spikes: a bramble is a three-sided tube whose every ring has one vertex pushed out 0.35..0.65 m, the side rotating ring to ring (a zigzag of thorns at six triangles a segment, rings every 1.1 m; no separate thorn geometry, which is what keeps 1140 plants inside the contract); 22 % are tall (6.5..8.5 m by the trunk down to 4..6 m at the bank, r 0.3..0.08, 60 % with a barbed side branch out of one stem quad), the rest scrub (4..6.5 m down to 2.5..4.5 m, r 0.2..0.08); all lean 0.3..0.7 of their height sideways and twist 20..90 deg as they climb so the crowns tangle, never inside r 8; highest tip y -2.8. Nothing on the bank, nothing over the rim. Rim and lane unchanged.
3. Guard eye y 28.7, 5.7 m over the lane, as map 1.
